Calendar designs

Three options for the Forex tab's economic calendar, using this week's real US events. Actual numbers fill in a few minutes after each release, marked as a beat or a miss against the forecast.

1Timeline

A vertical line through the day. Time on the left, numbers in one easy row, and what a beat or miss usually does.

2Scoreboard

Each release as a glowing card with big Actual · Forecast · Previous numbers and a clear beat or miss result.

3Clean list

One tidy row per event with the key number on the right. Tap a row to see all the numbers and what it usually moves.
