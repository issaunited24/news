Briefly, redesigned

Seven complete directions. Each one is a working app running on today's real news and real market data, not a picture.

Try each one: switch tabs (or swipe the feed), open Forex, then tap Gold, Dollar or Nasdaq to open the new chart. Drag your finger across the chart to read prices, and try all four chart types and the six ranges.
